## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Orders usually ship within 2 business days of being placed.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with UPS, FedEx, and DHL. When an order ships, we email a tracking number. You can also see it in Orders > Order details.

## Late packages

If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, or the package is 3 business days past the estimated date, contact support. We'll open a carrier investigation.
