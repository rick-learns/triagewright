## Pending authorizations

When you place an order, your bank may show a temporary authorization. It usually disappears within 3 to 5 business days. Only the final charge is collected.

## Double charges

If you see two final charges for the same order, contact support with the order number. The billing team will investigate and refund any duplicate charge.

## Invoices

You can download invoices from Orders > Order details > Invoice.
