## Turning on two-factor authentication

Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and scan the QR code with an authenticator app. Save your backup codes somewhere safe.

## Lost your device

Sign in with a backup code, then set up two-factor authentication again. If you've lost your backup codes too, contact support. The account security team will verify your identity before removing two-factor authentication.
