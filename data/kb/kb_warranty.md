## Coverage

All our electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects. It doesn't cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear.

## Making a claim

Contact support with the order number and a description of the problem. Warranty claims are handled by tier 2 support, who arrange a repair or replacement.
