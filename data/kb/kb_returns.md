## How to return an item

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from Orders > Return item. We'll email you a prepaid shipping label. Pack the item with all its accessories.

## After we receive it

We inspect returned items within 3 business days. Once the return is marked received, the refund for the returned item goes through the standard refund review.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.
