## Why accounts lock

An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row. This protects you from someone guessing your password.

## Unlocking

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page to get a reset link at the email on file. Resetting your password unlocks the account. The link expires after 1 hour.

## What support can't do

For your security, support can't unlock an account, change the email on file, or turn off two-factor authentication from a chat or email request. Those requests go to the account security team, who verify identity first.
