## Charging

Use the included cable or any certified USB-C cable. A full charge for the Aurora Headphones takes about 2 hours and gives up to 30 hours of playback.

## Overheating

If a device gets hot enough to be uncomfortable to touch, swells, or smells of burning, stop using it right away and unplug it. Contact support. We treat these reports as urgent safety issues.
