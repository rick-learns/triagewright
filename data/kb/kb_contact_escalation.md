## Asking for a human

You can ask to talk to a person at any time. We'll pass your conversation and a summary to a support specialist so you don't have to repeat yourself.

## Response times

Tier 1 replies within 1 business day. Billing and account security reply within 2 business days. Urgent safety issues, such as a device overheating, are handled first.
