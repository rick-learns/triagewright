## Arrived damaged

If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 30 days of delivery and describe the damage. A photo helps but isn't required. You don't need to send the item back for items under $50.00.

## Defective after use

Items that stop working within 30 days of delivery qualify for a refund. After 30 days, the 1-year limited warranty applies (see Warranty).
