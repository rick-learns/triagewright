## Who can get a refund

Delivered orders can be refunded within 30 days of the delivery date. The refund can't be more than the amount paid for the order, minus any amount we've already refunded.

Orders that haven't shipped yet should be cancelled instead of refunded. Cancelled orders are never charged.

## Limits

A support agent can't issue a single refund over $500.00. Larger refunds go to the billing team, who reply within 2 business days.

A customer can receive at most 2 refunds in any 30-day period. More requests go to the billing team for review.

## Approval

Every refund is reviewed by a team member before it's issued, usually within 1 business day. Once it's approved, the money reaches the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.

## Not refundable

Orders delivered more than 30 days ago, and orders that were never delivered, can't be refunded. Refund requests from closed or suspended accounts go to our account team for review.
