## Data access and deletion

You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account. Support passes these requests to the privacy team, who respond within 30 days as required by law.

## What we never ask for

We'll never ask for your password, full card number, or one-time codes by email or chat. Don't share them with anyone claiming to be support.
